How the Pipeline Works

From raw WAV audio to fingerprint comparison using LCS.

1

WAV Decoding

The WAV file is read and the audio samples, sample rate, and channels are extracted.

2

Audio to Matrix

The waveform is converted into a spectrogram and represented as a fixed 64 × 64 time-frequency matrix.

3

Matrix Compression

The 64 × 64 matrix is compressed into a 16 × 16 matrix while preserving the important energy patterns.

4

Frequency & Time Profiling

The compressed matrix is summarized into frequency-density and time-density profiles.

5

Fingerprint Generation

The frequency and time profiles are combined and compressed into a 16-character fingerprint.

6

LCS Comparison

The two fingerprints are compared using the Longest Common Subsequence algorithm.

7

Similarity Score

The LCS result is converted into the final similarity percentage between the two audio fingerprints.
